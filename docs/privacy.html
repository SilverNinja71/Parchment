Privacy Policy

Parchment for iPad · Effective October 25, 2026

Short version: Parchment does not collect, sell or share any of your data. Your notes stay on your iPad unless you choose to sync them to a cloud service you pick.

What stays on your iPad

Your notebooks, drawings, flashcards, study history and personalization drawings are saved only on your device. Handwriting recognition, math solving and handwriting search all run on the iPad using Apple's built-in technology. Your handwriting is never sent to us or to anyone else for recognition.

Optional sync

If you turn on sync, Parchment sends your notebooks directly from your iPad to the service and folder you choose: iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, a WebDAV server, or another storage app. The data goes straight to that service and is governed by its own privacy policy. Parchment has no servers and never receives a copy.

When you sign in to OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox, you sign in on that company's own page. Parchment receives an access token that lets it read and write files in your account, and stores it securely in your iPad's Keychain. Parchment never sees your password. For WebDAV, the server address, username and password you enter are stored in the Keychain on your iPad. Stopping sync and signing out removes them.

No tracking

Parchment contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party data-collection code.

Children

Parchment doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Parchment is open source, so you can check exactly what it does at github.com/SilverNinja71/Parchment.
